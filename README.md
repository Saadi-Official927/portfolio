## Products and Platforms

### BotnFlow
Multi tenant communication automation platform. Unified inbox across
WhatsApp Business API, Messenger, Instagram, webchat, Twilio SMS and
email, with an AI call centre layer on Twilio that qualifies callers,
books appointments into Google Calendar and hands off to a human when
needed. Also includes a lead generation engine that scrapes and
enriches business data through Apollo.

**Stack:** Node.js, React, MongoDB, Redis, Twilio, Meta Cloud API,
OpenAI, AWS
**Role:** Engineering across the messaging, voice and tenancy layers
**Live:** https://app.botnflow.com/

### BidUp
AI proposal tool for freelancers. Generates tailored Upwork proposals
from a job post using LLM APIs, monitors new jobs in real time with a
browser extension and notification pipeline, and tracks the pipeline
through to reply.

**Stack:** Next.js, Node.js, MongoDB, OpenAI, browser extension
**Live:** https://app.bidups.com

## Client Web Applications

### Halal Walls
Wallpaper platform built end to end. Dynamic Next.js frontend with SSR
and heavy SEO work, backed by a Node and Express API. Built from Figma
designs with a large amount of front end detail.

**Stack:** Next.js, Node.js, Express, MongoDB

### Harvestly Market
Multi tenant agricultural marketplace with three distinct portals.
Customers get cart checkout and local pickup scheduling, vendors get
inventory management, pre order forecasting and payout tracking, and
admins get vendor approvals and commission tracking. Real time stock
and pre order sync helps farmers plan pickup logistics.

**Stack:** Node.js, React, MongoDB, multi tenant architecture

### Omnia
Digital platform for boutique automotive insurance and asset
protection, aimed at luxury vehicle collectors and private clients.

**Stack:** Node.js, React, MongoDB

### Multi tenant CRM (white label)
Worked on an AI powered multi tenant CRM with unified customer data,
omnichannel messaging across email, SMS and WhatsApp, and automated
marketing workflows. My work focused on broadcasts and campaigns
through Meta WhatsApp templates and email delivery.

**Stack:** Node.js, MongoDB, Meta WhatsApp Business API

## AI and Automation Systems

### Bilingual AI voice receptionist
AI receptionist handling inbound patient calls in both Urdu and
English. Answers doctor availability questions against live schedule
data and books appointments end to end, with handoff to a human when
the caller needs one.

**Stack:** Twilio, OpenAI, Node.js, speech to text and text to speech

### Operations automation workflows
Production n8n workflows connecting project boards, CRMs and finance
systems. Form submissions become projects and CRM records, boards stay
in sync in both directions without loops, and won deals become
customer ready quotes and invoices.

**Stack:** n8n, Monday.com, 17hats, GoHighLevel, Xero, Shopify
**Repo:** https://github.com/Saadi-Official927/n8n-automation-workflows
